Addresses are normalized to lowercase by the application. The initial list is
seeded from AUTHORIZED_EMAILS at startup. Administrators can add/remove users
from the in-app Authorized users panel. Set ADMIN_EMAILS in Render to designate
at least one administrator; that address must also be in AUTHORIZED_EMAILS.

The application sends one-time PINs by SMTP. Configure SMTP_HOST, SMTP_PORT,
SMTP_USER, SMTP_PASS, and MAIL_FROM in Render. Do not commit real credentials.
